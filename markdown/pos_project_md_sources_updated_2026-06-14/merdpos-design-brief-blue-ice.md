MerdPOS Design System & Branding Brief: "Blue Ice" Concept
This document serves as the master design directive for the MerdPOS Point-of-Sale (POS) application, based on the approved A.1 "Blue Ice" logo concept. The core goal is to maintain a cohesive, sophisticated, "future-ready" tech aesthetic across all screens, states, and future marketing materials.
1. Core Visual Concept: "The Blue Ice Prism"
The brand identity is built on the concept of precision, security, and cool intelligence. The A.1 concept uses a multi-faceted geometric form that represents the complexity of modern retail operations being solved by clean, logical design.
* Key Descriptor: Cool, High-Tech, Crisp, Crystalline.
* The Icon (Detailed): The icon is an isometric projection of a hexagonal prism with complex internal geometric facets forming an 'M'. The A.1 variation uses subtle blue light-leaks emanating from the facets to create a "Blue Ice" glow, suggesting internal, automated intelligence.
2. Definitive Brand Elements (from Logo A.1)
All designs must strictly adhere to the elements from this specific logo iteration. Do not confuse with the other variants (B, C, or D).
2.1 Logo Presentation
The primary logo presentation is a horizontal lockup (icon on the left, wordmark on the right), presented on a dark background.
* Icon (Primary): The fully-rendered faceted prism with the blue ice glow.
* Wordmark:
o "Merd": Is a bold, clear, clean white sans-serif font. This must always appear pure white.
o "POS": Is a lighter-weight sans-serif font, rendered in a crisp, cool light blue. This blue must match the core glow color of the prism.
* Tagline:
o "FUTURE-READY RETAIL": Must appear below the wordmark, using the same cool light blue as "POS" but in a smaller, lighter-weight font. The text must be all-caps.

Application UI Guidelines
The aesthetic of the approved A.1 panel must be extended to the entire application.
3.1 Dark Mode First (Default State)
The app should be designed primarily in Dark Mode, replicating the depth and glow of the A.1 panel.
3.2 UI Elements & Accents
* Card Containers: Use deep background panels (like the #080C14) with sharp, glowing outlines in the Cool Blue or Gray, exactly as shown around the A.1 panel.
* Data Points & Graphs: All graphs and charts should use the Cool Blue and Accent Glow Blue from the prism as their primary data colors. Lines should be thin and crisp.
* Buttons & Interactivity: Use solid white or light-blue buttons. Critical action buttons should use the prism's faceted look or have a "glowing" hover state based on the Accent Glow Blue.
* Neural Overlays: Use subtle, faint overlays of neural network diagrams (the connected nodes and lines seen in the original image background) across background surfaces to imply AI intelligence.
3.3 Typography
The typography should be highly readable, precise, and tech-forward.
* Main Title Font: Replicate the main title font from "MerdPOS Logo Variations" (clean sans-serif).
* Label Font: Use the clear labels (A.1, Blue Ice Prism, etc.) as a guide for data labels.
* Wordmark Font: This must be consistent. Use a clean geometric sans-serif (like Montserrat or Metropolis) with the weight hierarchy seen in "MerdPOS".
3.4 Iconography
Any other icons in the application (e.g., cart, dashboard, inventory) should mirror the geometric, clean-line aesthetic of the prism.
* Style: Minimalist, wireframe, or slightly faceted, with subtle glowing blue accents. Avoid rounded, bubbly icons.
4. Design Tenets
* Maintain Depth: Utilize subtle glow, light leaks, and faceted geometry to create a sense of deep, active processing.
* Keep it Cool: The color temperature must remain strictly in the cool blue range. Do not introduce warm tones (no golds, oranges, or warm reds, even for errors—use a cooler error red/magenta).
* Future-Ready: The interface must feel advanced and AI-driven, not traditional. Avoid cluttered, boxy UI. Use negative space and clean lines.
